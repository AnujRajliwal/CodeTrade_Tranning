E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis Report
Objective

The objective of this project was to perform a complete Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist. The analysis aimed to identify sales patterns, customer behavior, product performance, seller contribution, payment trends, and regional performance to support data-driven business decision-making.

Dataset Description

The dataset consists of multiple related tables containing information about:

Customers
Orders
Order Items
Products
Sellers
Payments
Reviews
Geographic Information

The analysis required merging multiple datasets to create a complete business view of customer transactions and sales performance.

Data Cleaning Summary

Several data quality checks were performed before analysis:

Checked missing values across all datasets
Identified duplicate records
Converted date columns to datetime format
Validated table relationships using merge checks
Performed integrity testing on merged datasets
Verified successful joins between customers, orders, products, and sellers

These steps ensured that the analysis was based on accurate and reliable data.

Analysis Findings
Product Performance

A small number of product categories generated a significant share of total revenue, indicating strong concentration among top-performing categories.

Regional Performance

Sales were concentrated in specific states, suggesting that certain geographic markets contribute more heavily to overall business success.

Customer Analysis

Customer segmentation revealed that VIP customers generated significantly higher revenue than regular customers. Most customers were identified as one-time buyers.

Payment Analysis

Credit cards were the most commonly used payment method and contributed the highest share of transaction value.

Seller Analysis

A limited number of sellers accounted for a substantial portion of total marketplace revenue.

Customer Satisfaction

Review analysis showed generally positive customer feedback, although some categories received lower ratings and may require further investigation.

Sales Trends

Time-series analysis revealed fluctuations in sales volume across different periods, indicating potential seasonal purchasing behavior.

Visualizations Used

The project included multiple visualizations such as:

Revenue by Product Category
Sales by State
Customer Segment Analysis
Payment Method Comparison
Seller Revenue Rankings
Review Score Distribution
Monthly Revenue Trends
Seasonal Sales Analysis

These charts helped identify business trends and communicate findings effectively.

Final Conclusion

The analysis demonstrated that business performance is driven primarily by a small group of high-performing product categories, regions, customers, and sellers.

Key recommendations include:

Invest more resources in top-performing product categories
Strengthen customer retention initiatives
Expand marketing efforts in high-performing regions
Support high-value sellers through targeted programs
Improve low-rated product categories
Use seasonal trends to optimize inventory and promotional planning

Overall, the project successfully transformed raw transactional data into actionable business insights that can support strategic decision-making.
